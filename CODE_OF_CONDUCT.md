# How to behave round here

Its a balancer for a video game. This isnt complicated.

**Do:** ask questions, disagree about the code, say when somthings wrong, say when
somthings wrong with somthing I wrote. Blunt is fine. Blunt and specific is better.

**Dont:** have a go at somebody instead of their argument. No slurs, no harassment, no
dragging peoples clans or servers into it, no turning an issue into a place to settle
whatever happened in a match last week.

If somebody is being a problem, open an issue or say so in the thread. I'll either have a
word or remove them. Theres no committee, its just me, and I'd rather deal with it quickly
than have a process about it.

That applies to me as much as anybody. If I'm short with you in a thread and it wasnt
warranted, say so.
